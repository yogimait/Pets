# Marketing skill

## When to use

Use when the task needs audience-facing copy or positioning. Do not use before the product claim and evidence are settled.

## Inputs

- verified product facts
- audience and channel
- desired action
- proof: screenshots, benchmarks, contracts, shipped features
- brand voice, if one exists

## Procedure

1. State audience, channel, and goal.
2. List every claim the copy will make.
3. Check each claim against evidence.
4. Write the shortest copy that communicates value.
5. Remove hype, filler, invented urgency, and empty superlatives.
6. Produce variants only for a real difference in audience or channel.
7. Mark unverified claims before returning.

## Output

Use `prompts/shared/handoff.md`. Include copy, audience, channel, claims with evidence, and approvals needed.

## Checks

- no fake social proof, metrics, customers, or awards
- no future capability presented as shipped
- no guarantees the product cannot control
- no dark patterns
- no competitor claim without verification

## Boundaries

Do not publish, send, schedule, create accounts, impersonate the user, or use personal data without permission.
